Salam Ou Alaykom.
I am Hayat.

Born and raised in the heart of Morocco, my passion has always been sharing the soul of my homeland with the world. I am a native Moroccan woman with over 10 years of professional teaching experience at Study Arabic.

To me, teaching a language is about more than just vocabulary—it's about opening a door to our culture, our laughter, and making Arabic and Darija accessible to expats navigating their daily lives.

We'll talk, laugh, and learn. By the time we finish, you won't just speak Darija; you'll feel it.

  • 🌍 Fluent in Arabic, Darija, French, English and Tamazight
  • 📚 10+ years at Study Arabic
  • ☕ Cultural immersion focus
